MSIB stands for ==**Certified Internship and Independent Study**==, which is known in Indonesian as _Magang dan Studi Independen Bersertifikat_. It is a flagship program under the Kampus Merdeka initiative by the Indonesian Ministry of Education, Culture, Research, and Technology.
## Program Overview
- **Definition**: A government-backed program that bridges higher education with the professional industry.
- **Duration**: Lasts for one full semester (around 4 to 6 months).
- **Credit Conversion**: Activities can be converted into university credit units (SKS).

## Main Tracks
- **Certified Internship**: Real-world work experience inside partner companies and organizations.
- **Certified Independent Study**: Structured competency-based courses or projects solving real industry challenges.
