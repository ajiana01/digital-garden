A **Game Design Document (GDD)** is ==a living master blueprint that details every single component of a video game, including its core concept, mechanics, story, and art style==. It guides development teams from the early idea stage through production.

## Core Components of a GDD
- **Game Concept**:
    - Elevator pitch: a short 1-2 sentence summary of the game.
    - Genre and platform: what kind of game it is and where it runs (PC, mobile, console).
    - Target audience: who will play the game. [1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R_obRM_lFxs&vl=id)

- **Gameplay and Mechanics**:
    - Core loop: the repeating actions the player takes (e.g., explore, fight, collect).
    - Player controls and rules: how the character moves and interacts with the world.
    - Win and loss conditions: how a player succeeds or fails a level. [1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bro4rwYVo0I&t=162), [2](https://connect-prd-cdn.unity.com/20201215/83f3733d-3146-42de-8a69-f461d6662eb1/Game-Design-Document-Template.pdf), [3](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R_obRM_lFxs&vl=id)

- **World and Narrative**:
    - Story outline and lore.
    - Main characters and enemies.
    - Level design and environment flow. [1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hzPZznSmbao), [2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R_obRM_lFxs&vl=id), [3](https://ptik.unima.ac.id/en/blog/game-design-document-gdd-dalam-multimedia-blueprint-penting-pengembangan-game)

- **Art and Audio**:
    - Visual style, color palette, and concept art.
    - User interface (UI) layout and menus.
    - Sound effects and background music. [1](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/game-design-document-template-ammad-raza), [2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R_obRM_lFxs&vl=id)
